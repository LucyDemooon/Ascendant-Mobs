# Remnants: RPG Mobs 3.1.1

## Fixes

- **Health bars on Epic Fight mobs (Forge 1.20.1, NeoForge 1.21.1).** Epic Fight draws
  the mobs it animates (zombies, skeletons and others) with its own renderer, so the
  vanilla render pass where RPG Mobs draws its plates never ran for them, and their
  level, rank and health bar were missing. RPG Mobs now draws the plate for any mob
  whose vanilla render another mod takes over, without drawing a second plate when that
  mod also uses the vanilla path.

No gameplay, config or save changes.

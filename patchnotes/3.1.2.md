# Remnants: RPG Mobs 3.1.2

## Changes

- **Ice and Fire dragons, sea serpents and hydras are leveled and ranked.** RPG Mobs treated only vanilla monsters as
  hostile, and Ice and Fire's dragons and sea serpents count as animals, so they never got levels unless passive
  leveling was turned on. They are now leveled like other hostile mobs. Tamed dragons still are not.
- New entity tag `#remnant_mobs:hostile`: add any modded mob to it to have it leveled and ranked as hostile. The Ice
  and Fire entries are optional, so packs without Ice and Fire load as before.

No config or save changes. Mobs that already spawned keep their current state; newly spawned ones are leveled.
